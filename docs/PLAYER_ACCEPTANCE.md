**14 October 2026 · RTS Fleet Command 1.4.5**

Scope: the eight gameplay requests, subsequent missile/weapon/camera regressions, station tooltip and insignia issues, RTS-menu level restart, and the [philosophy divergences](PHILOSOPHY_AND_GAMEPLAY_RECOMMENDATIONS.md#divergences-from-the-philosophy--13-october-source-audit). Recovery means station service; withdrawal protection means covering a departing ship. There is no escape-pod collection, crew-transfer or rescue-ship requirement.

This contract supplements [AI acceptance and invariants](AI_ACCEPTANCE_AND_INVARIANTS.md). It defines future acceptance; it does not certify that the matrix below has been executed.

## What done means

A criterion is **DONE** only when its scenario and legitimate control pass on the retained code, its required evidence is recorded, and its shared-path regression checks pass. The original defect must be reproduced or the covering regression must demonstrably fail with the responsible fix removed. For an unreproduced reported crash, successful alternative scenarios establish limited coverage, not resolution of that incident.

Use **PENDING** for unfinished implementation/testing, **BLOCKED** for a named missing observation or engine capability, **FAILED** for an executed failure, and **DONE** for completed evidence. Keep implementation status separate from acceptance status. Do not replace an unknown with a pass, treat an observation cap as a draw, or count a skipped/missing test as exercised coverage.

Acceptance of the implemented scope is DONE when its G01–G11 and P01–P08 scenarios/controls pass, the final diff is reviewed, the automated gates below finish successfully, and the player-facing checks receive an explicit reviewer verdict. The implemented crew protection and broader/wing awards require their corresponding controls; scoped implementation is separate from broad gameplay acceptance. A limitation may close a criterion only after the user explicitly accepts that limitation; retain its evidence and scope. Committing code is a checkpoint, not gameplay acceptance or publication.

### Current crew model and controls

`FleetHumanity.ModelVersion=1` freezes each lifetime's complement at commission. Defaults are labeled role estimates: fighter 1, bomber 3, escort 80, capital 250, heavy 1,500, juggernaut 6,000, station 2,500. `ClassCrew` can override these as design estimates; no sourced canonical complements are claimed. Unknown classes stay unknown. Setup/intro actors and static defenses do not enter these totals.

Destruction estimates 80% of that complement dead (rounded); the remainder is missing/unconfirmed. Present/stranded hulls retain their complement aboard; confirmed native departure transfers it to escaped. This model does not simulate nonlethal crew casualties or escape pods. Repair cannot reduce historical dead/missing counts. Exact deaths are never claimed.

The top estimates strip is **hidden on every fresh game/restart**. **F9** toggles it in command or pilot view, including while paused; held-key repeats do not toggle again. Named losses and factual citations still use ordinary status notices. CREWS and the report retain estimate labels independently of this toggle.

Individual honors: RECOVERY_REJOIN and WITHDRAWAL_COVER retain their existing conditions. GALLANTRY requires removal of the same attacker that caused measured hostile projectile hull damage within eight mission seconds to an injured, servicing or withdrawing friendly lifetime. DISTINGUISHED_SERVICE requires five distinct hostile mobile lifetime kills over at least three minutes without observed friendly projectile damage. JUSTIFIED_SACRIFICE requires a lost recorded covering contributor followed by the beneficiary's confirmed escape or paid-service completion; death alone never qualifies. Wing protection requires at least two original contributing members; wing distinguished service requires five distinct victims, two original contributors, three minutes and no observed friendly projectile damage by original members. These are bounded observable rules, not proof of causation or moral worth; beams without equivalent damage evidence do not qualify gallantry.

Mod-owned buttons use normalized vector icons through the shared widget; variable configuration/price labels remain readable, and icon-only actions retain hover descriptions. Production thumbnails remain native ship images. This does not replace FSO's native menus or import SVG bitmap assets.

### Primary friendly fire and rare splinter raids

- Pilot non-beam primaries use the formal player as their native collision parent because FSO suppresses untargeted AI friendly laser damage. A signature map retains the actual firing hull for injury, kill credit, base protection and cleanup, including shots still flying after pilot exit. Secondary ownership remains on the possessed hull. Mission flags include NoTraitor so friendly injury does not convert the observer's faction.
- Rare raids can originate from any surviving configured faction. Existing ships never change sides. A hostile native Traitor hull or three-ship group uses that origin's unlocked fighter/bomber roster and warps in after a 12-second warning. Traitors are enemies of the player and their origin, retain an origin history, and receive attack orders during normal frame updates.
- Defaults: first chance after eight mission minutes; 2% roll every three mission minutes; at most one raid per match; 65% chance of three raiders rather than one. Pause/setup/intro/report/aftermath suspend events. Restart clears pending events and RNG state; losing the origin station before arrival cancels that arrival. These are unpurchased event ships, not ordinary production or CP expenditure.
- UI corrections from the annotated screenshots: no decorative corner glyphs on speed/map controls; no setup value/faction icons; no repeated mission-time note; text-only ALL/FLOWN/LOST/LIVE filters; single full-size policy action icons with hover labels; distinct armor, turret, logistics and command symbols. Sentry choices show tier and hull strength. Vector paths are code-native, not imported SVG textures.
- Follow-up screenshots: CREWS uses the archived ship class's retail icon (role silhouette only when unavailable), with lost row borders/names/fates and selected lost headings in red. Class/role remain available after native removal. Names and fate have separate columns so long names cannot hide the outcome. The panel ends after its content, hides unused single-page navigation and releases the reclaimed area to world input. Hover action labels retain configured shortcuts even with F10 hints hidden; SERVICE has no existing shortcut.
- Reset defaults both sky and music to RANDOM. Match start resolves a nebula from the existing 36-backdrop list; an explicit setup choice remains authoritative. Restart clears owned background handles and restores random defaults. Repeated random draws may legitimately choose the same backdrop.
