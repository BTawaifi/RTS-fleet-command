### Research and application

The June 2026 [HRL-IM/CBS preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30092) combines spatial influence, tactical scripts and coordinated clusters. Its StarCraft micromanagement experiments are not evidence of FSO balance. Here the transferable ideas are reach/arrival-weighted strength and coordinated escort assignments in the existing hierarchy; its Q-learning algorithm has not been implemented or trained.

[Society of Mind Meets RTS (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06042) separates specialized strategies from high-level selection. We retain the established operation phases instead of adding an external inference service. [SMACv2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.07489) shows why fixed scenarios can reward open-loop behavior: use state feedback, varied rosters and held-out seeds rather than treating one scripted match as proof. The author-released [UECD CoG 2026 project](https://github.com/mathisdelsart/microrts-drl-uecd) uses opponent curricula and multi-map evaluation; that informs the comparison workload, not a claim that its trained network runs in this mod.

### Mechanisms changed

- `UpdateAIProductionDemand` retains a 30-s half-life estimate of hostile mobile fleet composition. That contributes at most 0.35 total counter weight; current home contacts add up to 0.5 immediately. Dead/static/out-of-arena/passage-protected contacts are excluded through existing eligibility. This is a smoothed observation model, not learned policy weights. Existing AI awareness remains global; player fog does not limit it.
- `GetAICompositionTargets` normalizes baseline plus demand over unlocked, available roster roles. Classic fleets no longer aim for unavailable modern capital shares. Counter pressure keeps positive siege roles where the roster supports them. Existing CP/cap/payment/saving/request/emergency contracts remain.
- Local strength discounts late short-range arrivals over a 20-s horizon and ignores service/withdrawal assets. Existing imminent-engagement recovery uses its separate 6-s danger window; distant active long-range guns remain threats. This changes decisions rather than weapon damage or frame scheduling.
- A surplus idle home screen can escort a bomber after reserving home defense. Its assigned front follows the bomber. A full-planner regression exposed the incumbent-target score retaining the home station indefinitely; explicit escort assignment now bypasses that retention. Active defenders, local contacts, lure/probe and assault reservations remain excluded.
- An operation can continue while objective hull plus shields make net progress. The progress checkpoint requires at least 0.5% of target maximum hull (minimum one HP); self-healed chip damage does not continually renew it. No progress for `Operations.AttackSeconds` or loss of the strike force still causes regrouping. Emergency/service gaps pause the progress clock alongside the operation clock.
- The existing Vasudan Flux Cannon impact and both blast paths now use `RTS Static Artillery`. Command armor takes 2.5% instead of the capital-beam 25%; fleet armor retains 10%. Cannon reach and damage against ordinary ships remain. All factions share this rule. Retail uses the cannon on sentries; a MediaVP loadout that places that same weapon on a mobile ship also receives its artillery-specific station factor, and requires separate gameplay coverage.
- Strategic station defense and emergency purchases now have separate signals. Ready assigned defenders contribute role-matched value discounted by their arrival time over 30 seconds. Uncovered urgent contacts or critical home hull release savings for replacement production; an adequately covered contact still receives defense orders without continually resetting the bank. The matched GTI seed 8312 budget control/candidate both reach the 2,400-s cap; neither proves capital completion or increased win rate. The candidate reduces recorded station damage received, with changed unit mix and operation losses. Treat those outcomes as coupled evidence, not a unique causal attribution to every kill.
- Armed escorts count as siege assets when an operation loses bombers, matching `CanDamageBase`. Fighters that have no suitable hostile at the operation return to covering the strike anchor instead of being assigned a station they cannot damage. Exploitation still permits fighter interception of new mobile defenders. These regressions exercise the real operation selector and phase machine.
