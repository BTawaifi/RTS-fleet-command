FS1 ships and model assets
==========================

GTF Apollo, GTF Valkyrie, GTB Athena, PVF Anubis, and SB Shaitan and their
POF/map assets are from FreeSpace Port 3.7.3, files in `fsport3_7.vp`.

FSPort README, section 10, states: “All original FreeSpace material included
herein is copyright 1998-1999 Volition, Inc., Interplay, and THQ. Ported with
permission to FreeSpace 2 by Peter Oliver (Galemp) and Ian Warfield
(Goober5000). You may use these files for your own missions, campaigns, and
mods on a no-cost non-commercial basis with attribution.”

The bundled mod is non-commercial. FSPort VP files are not runtime
dependencies. The standalone ship table uses stock FS2 weapons; these
definitions preserve their hull/model identities and original handling,
reactor, afterburner and countermeasure values without loading FSPort's
ship or weapon tables. The restored values were read from that installed
archive's ships.tbl during the solo review.
