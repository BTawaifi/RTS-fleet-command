Faction insignias

rts-faction-gta.pcx, rts-faction-pvn.pcx, rts-faction-gtva.pcx,
rts-faction-ntf.pcx, rts-faction-hol.pcx and rts-faction-gti.pcx are
unchanged copies of the corresponding squad insignias in FreeSpace Port
3.7.3's fsport3_7.vp (data/players/squads).

see its readme.txt and https://wiki.hard-light.net/index.php/FreeSpace_Port.

The GTI insignia is the Port's badge based on the design created for Silent
Threat: Reborn, as documented in the Port's 3.1 release notes. Both loyal and
rogue GTI use it; their faction labels and team colors distinguish them.
PVN and the modern Vasudan Empire share the Vasudan insignia.
rts-faction-shivan.png is original mod artwork generated with the built-in
imagegen tool on 2026-10-06. It is a triangular arrangement of three red dots,
inspired by markings visible on some Shivan hulls. It is not an official or
canonical Shivan insignia. Transparent source pixels are preserved.

The original dark bronze GTVA art has a pale backdrop in the HUD for contrast;
the copied PCX itself is unchanged. The required insignia, model and texture
files are bundled with attribution; no FSPort tables, VP files or runtime
package dependency are loaded by this mod.
