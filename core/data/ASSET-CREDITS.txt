RTS Fleet Command asset credits
==============================

Original Lua/Python code, mission content and table definitions:
Boutros, 2026. MIT license; see LICENSE in the package root.
This grant excludes third-party assets and copied game materials.

FreeSpace Port ships, textures and insignias:
See FSPORT-ASSET-CREDITS.txt and maps/README-insignias.txt for their
authors, provenance and attributed non-commercial reuse terms.
The custom triangular Shivan insignia's origin is recorded there too.

Cairo station:
Bundled htl_cairo.pof and cairo_1, cairo_2, CairoTurrets, CairoLods and
matching debris PNGs were supplied by the owner, who identifies them as
public FreeSpace community assets.
The original low-poly Cairo is credited to Mike (Bobboau) Abegg; its later
lights texture is credited to Galemp by the FSMods archive:
https://www.freespacemods.net/download_476.html
That archived low-poly model is not the bundled HTL file. The exact HTL
author and redistribution terms have not been verified. Its embedded
metadata contains a PCS compiler stamp, not a license.

Arachnas station:
Bundled Arachnas.pof and sscv3b.pcx were supplied by the owner, who identifies
them as public FreeSpace community assets. Other referenced textures resolve
from retail FS2.
The exact bundled model's creator and redistribution terms are unverified.
The Cerberus Lost Years release credits a FreeSpaceFreak remake and grants
CC BY 3.0 for that pack, but its model differs from the bundled file; that
grant has not been assigned to this variant:
https://www.hard-light.net/forums/index.php?topic=91842.0

Promotional artwork:
artwork/logo.jpg, tile.jpg and banner.jpg were identified by the owner as
public FreeSpace community assets. No creator or redistribution terms were
recovered from repository records or embedded attribution metadata.

FreeSpace 2 and optional MediaVPs:
Retail game archives and MediaVP packages are external runtime dependencies,
not distributed in these payloads. Their respective owners retain their
rights. Optional MediaVP compatibility tables are not a grant over the
external package's models, textures or effects.

Public availability alone is not a recorded redistribution grant. The
unverified station/artwork provenance above remains a publication check.
